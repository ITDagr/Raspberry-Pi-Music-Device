# Raspberry-Pi-Music-Device


A Bluetooth music player based on the Raspberry Pi Zero
This is My ESP32 Music Device, which was focused on a simple goal, the ability to disconnect from society while still having access to music through Bluetooth headphones or a speaker. It is a primative prototype that lacks a battery, a proper case, and the overall feature set such as haptics and LED's. This was simply a proof of concept and firmware test and was my first experience into the world of AI software design.

BOM:
1.54' 240 x 240 display, 
Adafruit Ano Rotary Encoder
Pi-Beret
the Pi-Zero 2W
12 M2.5 Screws
Adafruit DRV2605L Haptic Control Board
Any LRA or Haptic Coin Style Motor

Limits: It currently functions on what i would consider is slow firmware with a plug needed to function. The raspberry Pi struggles with real timing and tends to lag behind inputs causing what feels like slow responses. In its current form factor a battery could not be squeezed in. The case is not optimal to keep it properly enclosed. Overall this is just version 1, and there is a lot more to come.

Other Software: This device ships with 2 sets of AI Designed software. 1 Runs the music engine as previously named, yet hte other one runs a simple local AI. This device is great for intresting handheld code on a very powerful mobile computer for anyone interested


What's Next- I have long planned a version 2 of this simple prototype i build. I plan to build it around an esp32 and custom PCB with a Teenage engineering style appearance and featureset that is more focused on music listeners instead of creators
